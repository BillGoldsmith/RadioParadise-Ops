add 127.0.0.1 localdev.radioparadise.com to /etc/hosts

getcerts.sh  will download certificates for localdev.radioparadise.com

launch devserver with certs on port 8000 to run locally without cors issues.

ng serve --ssl --host localdev.radioparadise.com --port 8000 --ssl-cert localdev/localdev_ssl/localdev.radioparadise.com/cert.pem --ssl-key localdev/localdev_ssl/localdev.radioparadise.com/privkey.pem
